# Historical Research: Master Sources & Citation Bibliography

## Tier 1 — Primary & Near-Primary Historical Records

1. **Imperial Gazetteer of India** (New Edition, 26 Volumes, Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1907–1909).
   - *Volume VII (Bareilly to Berhampore)*
   - *Volume VIII (Berhampore to Canal)*
   - *Volume XI (Dacca to Salde)*
   - *Volume XII (Salde to Gwalior)*
   - *Volume XX (Pushkar to Salween) — Provincial Series: Bengal (Vols. I & II, 1909)*
   - **Data Extracted**: Administrative division boundaries, district areas, 1901 census population, district headquarters, local trade, river courses.

2. **Census of India, 1901: Volume VI — The Lower Provinces of Bengal and their Feudatory States** by E.A. Gait, I.C.S. (Calcutta: Bengal Secretariat Press, 1902).
   - **Data Extracted**: Official district spelling, population numbers, sub-divisional lists, religious breakdown, dialect distribution.

3. **Bengal District Gazetteers Series** (edited by L.S.S. O'Malley, W.W. Hunter et al., Bengal Secretariat Book Depot, 1906–1915).
   - *24-Parganas* (1914)
   - *Murshidabad* (1914)
   - *Dacca* (1912 by B.C. Allen)
   - *Chittagong* (1908 by L.S.S. O'Malley)
   - *Darjeeling* (1907 by L.S.S. O'Malley)
   - *Midnapore* (1911 by L.S.S. O'Malley)
   - *Puri* (1908 by L.S.S. O'Malley)
   - *Santal Parganas* (1910 by L.S.S. O'Malley)

4. **Survey of India Maps (1890–1905)**
   - *Map of the Province of Bengal showing Administrative Divisions and Districts* (Calcutta: Survey of India Offices, 1904).
   - *Imperial Atlas of India: Bengal Sheet Series* (1898–1905).

## Tier 3 — Structured Reference Data & Media Provenance

1. **Wikidata (CC0 1.0 Universal)**
   - Used for coordinate verification, P625 geolocation, P17 country, P131 administrative entity, and P1448 official/historical names.
2. **Wikimedia Commons (Public Domain / CC-BY-SA 3.0 / CC-BY-SA 4.0)**
   - Used for verified historical photographs (1860–1910 photogravures, John Johnston prints, Bourne & Shepherd photographs), maps, and illustrations.
3. **GeoNames (CC BY 4.0)**
   - Used for modern coordinate confirmation and physical feature verification.

## Confidence Rating Classification

Every record in the Bengal Through Time datasets is assigned a confidence rating:

- **HIGH**: Corroborated by at least two independent Tier 1 historical sources (e.g. Imperial Gazetteer 1907 + Survey of India 1904 map + 1901 Census).
- **MEDIUM**: Supported by one Tier 1 source and corroborated by Tier 2 scholarship or Wikidata coordinates.
- **LOW**: Inferred from near-contemporary regional descriptions or modern geography where historical boundaries shifted.
- **UNCERTAIN**: Historical location or administrative alignment has conflicting claims in primary sources; explicitly annotated in UI.
